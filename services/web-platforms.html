Web Platforms

Web Platform and SaaS Development

We build websites and web platforms that load quickly, are easy to find and can grow with your business, from a corporate site to a customer portal or a full SaaS product.

Start a Project Try Fat Build
In short

A web platform is a website that does more than show information: it lets people log in, manage an account, buy, book or work with their own data. At Fat Elephant we build corporate websites, customer portals, SaaS platforms and headless CMS set-ups that work on every screen and are structured so search engines can read them.

What We Do

What we do: Web Platforms

Six kinds of web work, from a single site to a platform with accounts, data and content tools behind it.

Corporate websites

Sites that explain what you do clearly and make it easy to get in touch. We care about speed, plain language and a design that suits your brand, because the site is often the first thing a customer sees.

Customer portals

A secure place where customers or partners can sign in to see their orders, documents or account details, and get things done without sending an email. We design them around the few tasks people come back for.

SaaS platforms

Software that people use through the web, with accounts, roles and subscriptions behind it. We help you build a first version that works, then grow it as real users show you what they need.

Headless CMS

A content system that is separate from the front end, so your team can edit pages and publish without a developer, and the site stays fast and flexible. The same content can later feed an app or another site.

Responsive development

Every page is built to work on phones, tablets and large screens, not only squeezed down from a desktop version. People should have a good experience whichever device they arrive on.

SEO-ready architecture

We build in the structure search engines look for: clean page layouts, fast loading, descriptive titles and headings, structured data and sitemaps. It gives your content a fair chance of being found.

The Process

How it works

Three phases, one team. The people who plan the site are the people who build it, and they stay on after launch.

  1. 01

    Shape it

    We agree who the site is for, what it has to do and how it should be organised. We map the pages and flows first, so the build starts from a plan and not a guess.

  2. 02

    Build it

    We design and build together, sharing working pages early. You see the real thing taking shape, on real devices, and can steer as we go.

  3. 03

    Launch and grow

    We check everything carefully before launch, then take the site live and keep an eye on how it performs. After launch we can keep improving it as your business and your audience change.

Honest Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit if you are

  • Businesses whose current site is slow, dated or hard to update
  • Companies that need a portal for customers, partners or staff
  • Founders building a SaaS product who want it done properly from the start
  • Marketing teams who want to publish content without waiting for a developer

Probably not for

  • Simple one-page sites where a ready-made website builder would do. We'll tell you when that is the case
  • Projects that need a native mobile app first. For that, see our Engineering work
  • Anyone who needs a promise of the top spot on Google. Nobody can honestly offer that, because rankings depend on many things, including your content
FAQ

Questions we get asked

What is the difference between a website and a web platform?

A website mostly shows information. A web platform lets people do things: sign in, manage an account, place an order or work with their own data. Many businesses need a good website first and a platform later.

What is a headless CMS?

A headless CMS is a content system with no built-in front end. Your team edits content in one place, and the website, an app or anything else pulls it in. It tends to give faster sites and more freedom in how the content is used.

What does SEO-ready mean?

It means the site is built with the structure search engines need: clean markup, fast loading, clear titles and headings, structured data and sitemaps. We cannot promise rankings, since those also depend on your content and your competitors.

Will the site work well on phones?

Yes. We build for small screens as carefully as for large ones, and we test on real devices, because most visitors will arrive on a phone.

Can we update the content ourselves?

Yes. We set up the content tools so your team can edit and publish without a developer, and we show your team how to use them.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

It depends on how many pages, what the platform has to do and how much needs to be connected. Tell us what you are trying to build and we will suggest the right starting point, with a clear scope agreed before any work begins.

Have an idea worth building?

Tell us what you are trying to build, and we will suggest the right place to start.
