In short

AI solutions development is the work of applying language models, agents and automation to a specific business problem, and connecting them to the tools and data you already use. At Fat Elephant we start with the problem, check that AI is the right answer, and build only what you will actually use.

What We Do

What we do: AI Solutions

Six kinds of AI work, from a single feature to a full product. We start with whichever one fits your problem, not whichever is fashionable.

AI agents

Software that can plan, use tools and complete multi-step tasks on its own, within limits you set. We design the task, the guardrails and the handover to a person for the moments when the agent is not sure.

Workflow automation

We find the repetitive steps that eat your team's time, such as sorting, drafting, checking and routing, and automate the ones where a machine is reliable enough. The rest stays with people, where it belongs.

LLM integrations

We connect language models to your product, your data and your systems: summarising, answering questions, pulling information out of documents. We choose the model and the set-up to fit the job, not the other way round.

Custom AI applications

When the idea is bigger than a feature, we design and build a full AI-powered product, from the interface to the back end, with the same care we give to any product we ship.

AI strategy

A clear view of where AI will help your business, where it won't, and what to try first. We rank ideas by value and risk, and we say so plainly when a simpler solution is the better one.

Intelligent search

Search that understands what people mean, across your documents, help centre or product catalogue, with answers that point back to the sources so people can check them.

The Process

How it works

Three phases, one team. The people who scope the work are the people who build it, and they stay on after launch.

  1. 01

    Find the real problem

    We start with the task and the people who do it, not the technology. We map the workflow, the data and what a good result looks like, and we decide honestly whether AI is the right tool.

  2. 02

    Build it and test it properly

    We build a working version quickly, then test it on real examples, including the awkward ones. We measure where it fails, add guardrails, and put a human check in wherever a wrong answer would be costly.

  3. 03

    Launch it and keep it honest

    We ship it into the tools your team already uses, watch how it behaves and keep tuning it. Models change and data drifts, so we plan for upkeep from the start instead of handing over a one-off build.

Honest Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit if you are

  • Teams with a specific, repetitive or knowledge-heavy task they want to do better
  • Product teams adding AI features to a product that already exists
  • Founders building an AI-first product who need a team to build it properly
  • Businesses that want an honest opinion on whether AI is worth it

Probably not for

  • Projects that start with "we need AI" and no problem attached
  • Use cases where a wrong answer is costly and nobody will ever check the output
  • Anyone expecting a model to be right every time. We'll say plainly when a simpler tool or a change in process would serve you better
FAQ

Questions we get asked

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that uses a language model to work towards a goal. It can plan steps, use tools such as search or your internal systems, and complete tasks with limited supervision. Good agents have clear limits and hand over to a person when they are unsure.

How do you decide whether AI is the right solution?

We look at the task, the data and the cost of a wrong answer. If a simpler, rule-based tool does the job reliably, we will recommend that. AI earns its place when the task is fuzzy, language-heavy or too varied for fixed rules.

Which models do you use?

We choose the model for the job, looking at quality, speed, cost and where your data is allowed to go. We explain the choice in plain language, and we avoid tying you to a single provider where we can.

How do you keep AI output accurate and safe?

We test on real examples, ground answers in your own data where possible, add guardrails, and keep a person in the loop for anything high-stakes. After launch we keep watching how it behaves.

Can you add AI to a product we already have?

Often, yes. Many AI features are an integration into an existing product rather than a rebuild. Our engineers handle the back end and the interface, so the feature fits the product you already have.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

It depends on the problem, the data and how much has to be connected. Tell us what you are trying to solve and we will suggest a sensible first step, with a clear scope agreed before any work begins.

Tell us what you are trying to build, and we will suggest the right place to start.
