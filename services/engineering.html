Custom Software Engineering: Web, Mobile and Backend

In short

Software engineering is the work of turning a design into a working product: the screens people use, the servers and data behind them and the connections to other systems. At Fat Elephant it covers web and mobile applications, backend systems, APIs and integrations, performance optimisation and quality assurance.

What We Do

What we do: Engineering

Six areas of engineering work, used together or on their own. Most products need several of them at once.

Mobile applications

Apps for the phones your users carry, built to feel fast and natural to use. We plan for the details that make or break a mobile product, such as patchy connections, small screens and different devices.

Web applications

Applications that run in the browser, from a focused internal tool to a full customer-facing product. They are built to be quick, accessible and simple to extend as your needs change.

Backend systems

The part users never see: servers, databases and business logic that hold everything together. We design it to be dependable and clear, so it can grow without being rewritten.

APIs and integrations

We connect your product to the other tools you rely on, such as payments, messaging, accounting or the systems you already run, and we design clean APIs so other products can connect to yours.

Performance optimisation

Slow software loses users. We measure where time is being lost, in the code, the database or the network, and fix the causes rather than the symptoms.

Quality assurance

Testing is part of the work, not a step at the end. We check as we build, test the awkward cases as well as the happy path and fix problems before they reach your users.

The Process

How it works

Three phases, one team. The people who scope the work are the people who build it, and they stay on after launch.

  1. 01

    Plan the build

    We start from the scope, the designs and the constraints. We agree the architecture and the order of work, so the first stage is clear and the risky parts are tackled early.

  2. 02

    Build in small steps

    We ship in regular, small releases that you can see and try, rather than one big reveal at the end. Feedback shapes the next step, and testing happens as we go.

  3. 03

    Release and look after it

    We take the product live carefully, watch how it behaves and fix what we find. We stay on to keep improving it, or hand it over cleanly if you would rather carry on with your own team.

Honest Fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit if you are

  • Founders with a scoped first version that is ready to build
  • Product teams that need extra engineering capacity they can trust
  • Companies with an older system to modernise or connect to newer tools
  • Teams who want clear, well-organised code their own developers can read

Probably not for

  • Projects with no scope yet. Start with Product Strategy, so we know what we are building
  • Anyone who wants the cheapest possible build whatever the quality
  • Teams who expect us to promise a fixed outcome before we have seen the problem. We'll look first, then tell you honestly what it needs
FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do you build both web and mobile?

Yes. We build web applications, mobile applications and the backend systems behind them, and we can do all three as one project so they work together from the start.

Can you work on code that already exists?

Often, yes. We start by reading it and telling you honestly what state it is in, then agree whether to extend it, tidy it or replace the parts that hold you back.

How do you make sure the software is reliable?

We test as we build, including the awkward cases, and we release in small steps so problems are easy to find. After launch we keep watching how the product behaves.

Can our own developers work with your code?

Yes. We build on clean architecture and keep the code clear, so your developers can read it, review it and work alongside ours, or take it over.

How will we know how the build is going?

You will see working software in regular, small releases, so you are judging the real thing and not a progress report. You can steer at every step.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

It depends on what is being built and how much has to be connected. Tell us what you are trying to build and we will suggest the right starting point, with a clear scope agreed before any work begins.

Have an idea worth building?

Tell us what you are trying to build, and we will suggest the right place to start.
