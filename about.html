01

We say the quiet part out loud. Honest feedback on your idea beats polite silence — even when it's not what you were hoping to hear.

02

Meetings end with decisions. Not homework, and not "let's take this offline" forever.

Slides are optional. Shipping isn't. We'd rather hand you a working prototype than a beautiful deck.
